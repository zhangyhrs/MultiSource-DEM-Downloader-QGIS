about=Multi-Source DEM Downloader is a QGIS plugin for downloading and processing multiple global elevation datasets. It supports public Copernicus DEM GLO-30/GLO-90 tiles, NASA Earthdata products such as SRTM and NASADEM, and JAXA AW3D30 with registered-user authentication. Study areas can be defined from the current map extent, an interactively drawn rectangle, polygon layers, or external SHP/GPKG/GeoJSON files. The plugin can mosaic, clip, load results into QGIS and optionally generate hillshade.
